💡 Reflected XSS Mechanics

In Reflected XSS, the payload travels from the HTTP request (URL parameter or form input) directly into the immediate HTTP response without being permanently stored in any database. The attacker typically embeds the payload in a phishing URL.

Mitigation: Context-aware output encoding (converting <, >, " to HTML entities) and Content Security Policy (CSP).
